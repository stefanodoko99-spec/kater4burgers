# Home page overrides

These rules override the generated master for the current Kater Burgers homepage.

- Brand palette: white, Kater blue `#244ac8`, and deep navy `#0b1d56`.
- The official wide Kater Burgers logo must keep its original `1200 × 282` aspect ratio; never crop, stretch, or place it on a low-contrast fill.
- The hero begins directly with the assembled layered Classic burger. Do not show a separate full-product photo or a burger selector before the scroll story.
- The scroll sequence remains focused on the burger only: full burger, expanded ingredients, then full burger again. Do not add a box, fries, or standalone sauce cups.
- The Classic build uses the generated transparent PNG layers from `public/images/ingredients/classic-generated/final/`.
- On desktop, the ingredient stack starts assembled on the right, remains fixed there, expands only vertically, rebuilds, then drops below the hero to hand off to the menu. All story information stays on the left.
- Keep the assembled burger slightly smaller than the surrounding editorial copy and use compact, visually even spacing between its layers.
- Typography uses DM Sans for the clean rounded editorial voice and Caveat only for expressive accent lines, echoing Instagram's refreshed bold-and-personal wordmark direction without copying its proprietary type assets.
- Seasonal colors may appear only as subtle secondary detail; primary surfaces stay white or blue.
- Keep all scroll animation on transform and opacity, retain keyboard focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
