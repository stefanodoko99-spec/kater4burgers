# Home page overrides

These rules override the generated master for the current Kater Burgers homepage.

- Brand palette: white, Katër blue `#244ac8`, and deep navy `#0b1d56`, with one restrained ingredient-inspired accent for each burger.
- The official wide Katër Burgers logo must keep its original `1200 × 282` aspect ratio; never crop, stretch, or place it on a low-contrast fill.
- The hero begins directly with the four-burger scroll story. Do not place a separate product photograph or selector before it.
- The pinned sequence is a four-burger relay: Bloom, Jalapeños, Oklahoma, and Classic. Each enters assembled, expands vertically into supplied ingredient layers, rebuilds, and hands off in place.
- Keep the burger stage anchored on the right on desktop and the story copy on the left. On mobile, place the burger above the active copy with no horizontal drift or overflow.
- The complete burger must remain compact, and the expanded layers must use visually even spacing.
- Typography uses Outfit throughout for a bold geometric editorial voice. Use weight and scale—not a decorative script—to create hierarchy.
- Copy must describe only the four burgers, their ingredients, the cooking approach, and the Tirana location. Do not add unrelated product stories.
- Keep scroll animation on transform and opacity, maintain visible keyboard focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
